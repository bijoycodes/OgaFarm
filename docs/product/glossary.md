# OgaFarm — Glossary

| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Agrofarmer | Same role as Farmer. A person or business producing crops, livestock, fish, eggs and similar |
| Availability state | Available now, Expected soon, Recurring supply, Seasonal, Reserved, Sold out, Temporarily unavailable |
| Business category (storefront tab) | One of the five seller-configurable tabs that organise a storefront |
| Buyer need / RFQ | A buyer's published requirement (product, quantity, location, deadline) that sellers quote against |
| Confirmed stock | Quantity a seller has confirmed, with a timestamp. Compare with *forecast stock* |
| Derica / Mudu / Paint bucket | Common Nigerian market volume measures, used as sale units |
| Enquiry | A question or quote request. Not an order |
| Entitlement | Server-maintained right to paid-role features, backed by the payment ledger |
| Forecast stock | Expected future quantity (e.g. harvest). Always labelled as a forecast |
| Group order | A pooled purchase by 4 or more buyers, each paying their own share |
| Matching | Rule-based pairing of buyer needs with eligible sellers by product, location, quantity, timing and stock freshness |
| OG preview | Open Graph title, image and description shown when a link is shared |
| `paid_until` | End of the paid subscription period. The access end date |
| Proof of delivery | Receiver confirmation, plus the quantity and condition recorded at delivery |
| Rescue produce | Perishable, visually imperfect or irregular-size produce sold at a fixed discounted price with condition disclosure |
| Section | Top-level product grouping: Crops & Fresh Produce, Livestock & Animal Products, Bulk Produce & Other Farm Goods, Farm Inputs & Equipment |
| Storefront | A seller's public page: `/farm/{slug}` or `/supplier/{slug}` |
| Verified owner | The real person or business identity behind one or more accounts |
| WAT | West Africa Time (UTC+1), the display time zone |

## Product taxonomy

| Section | Categories |
|---|---|
| Crops & Fresh Produce | Vegetables, Fruits, Grains & Cereals, Tubers & Roots, Legumes & Beans, Herbs & Spices |
| Livestock & Animal Products | Poultry & Live Birds, Goats & Sheep, Cattle & Other Livestock, Fish & Aquaculture, Eggs, Dairy & Animal Products |
| Bulk Produce & Other Farm Goods | Processed & Packaged Foods, Bulk Agricultural Produce, Other Edible Produce |
| Farm Inputs & Equipment | Seeds & Seedlings, Fertiliser, Crop protection, Feed & livestock supplies, Day-old chicks, Equipment & tools, Packaging |

The marketplace may also expose browse shortcuts: Fresh Produce, Dry Produce, Dried Fruits, Livestock.
